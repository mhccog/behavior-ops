# Credits

## 3D figure ("Show me in 3D")
- **The figure itself** is built in code from simple shapes (spheres, capsules and lathe shapes) in `figure/viewer.js`.
  It was made for this site. No third-party 3D model, photo, texture or motion capture is used, so there is nothing to license.
- **Poses and animations** for each cue are hand-written keyframes in `figure/cues3d.js`, based on our own summaries of the cues.
- **three.js** (r170) renders the scene. It is © three.js authors under the [MIT licence](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/LICENSE)
  and is loaded from the jsDelivr CDN only when a figure is opened. OrbitControls comes from the same package.
